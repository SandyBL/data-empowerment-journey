Stewardship is the mechanism that turns authority into effect. A council can decide that "revenue" has one definition; stewardship is what finds the four systems where it is calculated differently, gets three of them changed, and documents the fourth as a known exception. As a discipline it is mostly unglamorous: maintaining a glossary, chasing an issue across two teams, explaining the same definition to a new analyst for the fifth time.

**In practice.** A stewardship model names, per domain, who the owner is, who the stewards are, how a definition change is proposed and approved, and where issues are logged. Written down, that is two pages, and those two pages are the operating model most programs are missing.

**Where it goes wrong.** Stewardship gets treated as documentation work and measured by glossary term count. Term count is a vanity metric — a glossary of 900 terms nobody looks at is worth less than 40 terms that the reporting team actually uses. Measure use and resolution, not volume.
