Every download on this site is free, and none of it is behind a form. You can take the templates and never hear from me again, which is the arrangement I would want.

There is a newsletter at the bottom of this page. It is optional and it does not unlock anything, because a resource you have to trade an email address for is not free: it is priced in a currency that is hard to see.

## The tools

**[Cost of bad data calculator](/en/calculator/)**: puts a defensible number on what your current data problems cost per year. Runs in the browser, nothing is sent anywhere. This is usually the fastest route to a funded governance conversation, because it converts "our data is bad" into a figure a CFO can argue with.

**[Maturity assessment](/en/maturity-assessment/)**: a practical DAMA-based scorecard, about fifteen minutes, which returns your maturity level, a five-axis radar chart and three quick wins focused on your weakest pillar.

**[Template library](/en/templates/)**: the Excel and Word templates I actually use on engagements: pain-point identification, a 5W2H governance framing, governance evolution stages, catalog tool use cases, principles and policies, and a governance scorecard.

**[Playbooks](/en/playbooks/)**: two PDF playbooks, one on standing up data governance and one on AI governance, plus the maturity assessment framework as a document.

**Scenario simulators**: three branching scenarios that put you inside the trade-offs instead of describing them: [Data Governance Day-to-Day](/simulators/en/data-governance-day-to-day/), [Who Owns This?](/simulators/en/data-ownership-conflict/) and [Data Literacy](/simulators/en/data-literacy/). If you want to run these with your own team, on a private leaderboard and rewritten around your systems, that is the [workshop format](/en/workshops/). The published runs, and the lessons that come out of them, are summarized on [the simulator results page](/en/simulator-results/).

## Where to start

If you do not know what is wrong, start with the [maturity assessment](/en/maturity-assessment/). If you know what is wrong and need budget, start with the [calculator](/en/calculator/). If you have budget and need to produce something next week, start with the [templates](/en/templates/).

And if you would rather read than download, the [glossary](/en/glossary/) defines the vocabulary and the [articles](/en/blog/) work through the arguments.
