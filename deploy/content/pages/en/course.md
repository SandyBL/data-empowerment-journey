{{COURSE_COVER}}

There is plenty of good material on data governance. Most of it is written for companies that already have a Chief Data Officer, an approved budget and three people dedicated to the subject. None of it answers the question that lands on your desk: how you put together the first policy, who you invite to the committee, and what you show at the next meeting so you do not lose your sponsor.

This course is that part. It follows the order a programme is really built in, with the artefacts I open on client projects.

You finish with more than theory: you leave with a strategy and a clear path to follow to implement data governance in your company, or to improve the one you already have — what to do first, with whom, with which artefacts and how to tell whether it is working.

The method is the **Data Governance Journey methodology**, the same one [set out on this site](/en/#framework), based on and guided by the **DAMA-DMBOK** framework, DAMA International's body of knowledge for data management. The course's three sections are the methodology's three pillars — the Compass, the Engine and the Heart — and each lesson takes what the DMBOK describes into the practice of a real programme.

**Before anything else:** the course is in **Portuguese**, the language it was made in, and the videos have **English subtitles**. The templates and downloadable materials inside the course are in Portuguese too. If that works for you, read on; if not, everything free on this site is in English and you do not need the course to use it.

## Why a course, when this site is free

Everything free here stays free: the [templates](/en/templates/), the [playbooks](/en/playbooks/), the [cost calculator](/en/calculator/), the [maturity assessment](/en/maturity-assessment/), the simulators, and fifteen years of lessons learned the hard way spread across the [articles](/en/blog/). Nothing is behind a form and nothing is going to be.

What a free tool cannot do is stay with you. It hands you a spreadsheet and leaves you alone in a meeting room with nine people from four departments who disagree about what a customer is. The course is the order, the context and the decisions: why this artefact before that one, what to do when the business says it has no time, how to score the criticality of a data element without turning it into a six-month project, and how to notice that your committee has turned into a status meeting before it dies of boredom.

## Who this is for

For the people who will implement. Analysts and managers who have been handed "set up governance", business people who became owners of a data domain without asking, data teams that have a platform and no agreement, and consultants who need artefacts that survive contact with an executive board. The first section starts from zero, so you do not need to know anything already — you need a real problem to apply it to, and to be comfortable watching lessons in Portuguese with subtitles.

**Who this is not for.** If you want to prepare for the DAMA exam, the route is different: the [CDMP exam practice](/simulators/en/cdmp-exam-practice/) is free and in English, like the exam. If you are looking for a tool, this course is deliberately vendor-neutral. And if what you need is someone inside your project, in English, that is [advisory](/en/advisory/) or a [workshop](/en/workshops/), not a video course.
