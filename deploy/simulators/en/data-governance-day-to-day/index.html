About this simulator

The Data Governance Day-to-Day Simulator is a free, browser-based exercise that puts you in the seat of the person who has to answer ten governance incidents in a row — the kind that arrive as a Slack message rather than as a project. Marketing wants the "Active Customer" formula changed because their numbers disagree with Sales. A stale table is quietly costing five figures a month in cloud spend. New data scientists spend weeks finding datasets that already exist. Each incident offers three plausible responses, and none of them is free.

Every choice moves five DAMA-DMBOK indicators — Efficiency, Trust, Accountability, Security, and Context — and draws down a fixed budget. The point is the trade-off: the fastest fix usually costs accountability, and the most rigorous one usually costs budget and goodwill. After the tenth incident the simulator scores the pattern of your decisions rather than any single answer, and explains which DMBOK principle each one leaned on.

It takes about ten minutes and needs no sign-up. Your decisions are scored in your browser and stay there. The leaderboard is the one exception: if you choose to publish your result, the display name you type and your score are saved so every other player can see them.

What you practise

  • Roles and responsibilities — deciding when a dispute needs a named data steward rather than another meeting.
  • Data quality management — choosing between filtering bad data at the report and fixing it at the source.
  • Metadata and data lineage — what it costs to leave a business glossary unwritten.
  • Data catalog and discovery — why a wiki page is not a catalog.
  • Master and reference data — resolving duplicate customers across CRM and sales systems.
  • Data lifecycle and retention — deprecating data without breaking the reports that depend on it.
  • FinOps and analytics governance — query quotas, cost attribution, and who pays for an inefficient join.

Questions about this simulator

Do I need to know DAMA-DMBOK to use it?

No. Each scenario is written in plain operational language, and the debrief after every decision names the DMBOK principle behind it. Many people use the simulator as their first introduction to the framework rather than as a test of it.

Is there a single correct answer to each scenario?

No. Every option is one a real organisation has chosen. The scoring reflects what each option costs and buys across the five indicators, so a defensible decision under budget pressure can score well even when it is not the textbook answer.

Is it free, and does it collect my data?

It is free and requires no account. Your decisions are scored in your browser and never leave it. Publishing to the leaderboard is optional and the only thing that is sent to us: the display name you type and your final score, which then appear on the same global board every other player sees.

What does the final score mean?

The score is a weighted maturity index across Efficiency, Trust, Accountability, Security, and Context, plus the budget you have left. It is a reading of your decision-making style, not a certification or a benchmark of your organisation.

DAMA-DMBOK Framework Reference

Core Areas for Enterprise Data Governance

The DAMA-DMBOK2 framework structures data management into 11 interconnected functions with Data Governance as the central orchestrator.

1. Data Governance

Planning, oversight, and control over data management and strategy.

2. Data Security & Privacy

Ensuring confidentiality, access control, regulatory compliance (GDPR/CCPA), and encryption.

3. Metadata & Context

Business glossaries, data catalogs, technical definitions, and end-to-end lineage.

4. Data Quality

Defining, measuring, monitoring, and improving data fitness for use.

5. Master & Reference Data

Single customer/entity records, identity resolution, and enterprise taxonomies.

6. Data Architecture

Structural design, semantic layers, and FinOps cloud cost management.
