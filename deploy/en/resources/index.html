Free tools

Everything here is free and needs no email

Templates, playbooks, a cost calculator, a maturity assessment and three scenario simulators. No gate, no form, no follow-up sequence.

Last updated September 5, 2026

Every download on this site is free, and none of it is behind a form. You can take the templates and never hear from me again, which is the arrangement I would want.

There is a newsletter at the bottom of this page. It is optional and it does not unlock anything, because a resource you have to trade an email address for is not free — it is priced in a currency that is hard to see.

The tools

Cost of bad data calculator — puts a defensible number on what your current data problems cost per year. Runs in the browser, nothing is sent anywhere. This is usually the fastest route to a funded governance conversation, because it converts "our data is bad" into a figure a CFO can argue with.

Maturity assessment — a practical DAMA-based scorecard, about fifteen minutes, which returns your maturity level, a five-axis radar chart and three quick wins focused on your weakest pillar.

Template library — the Excel and Word templates I actually use on engagements: pain-point identification, a 5W2H governance framing, governance evolution stages, catalogue tool use cases, principles and policies, and a governance scorecard.

Playbooks — two PDF playbooks, one on standing up data governance and one on AI governance, plus the maturity assessment framework as a document.

Scenario simulators — three branching scenarios that put you inside the trade-offs instead of describing them: Data Governance Day-to-Day, Who Owns This? and Data Literacy. If you want to run these with your own team, on a private leaderboard and rewritten around your systems, that is the workshop format. The published runs, and the lessons that come out of them, are summarised on the simulator results page.

Where to start

If you do not know what is wrong, start with the maturity assessment. If you know what is wrong and need budget, start with the calculator. If you have budget and need to produce something next week, start with the templates.

And if you would rather read than download, the glossary defines the vocabulary and the articles work through the arguments.

Optional
