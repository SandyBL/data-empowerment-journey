Action Plan

3 Immediate Steps to Reclaim Your Budget

Operational fixes to capture up to 60% of lost hours within 30 days without expensive software rollouts

Step 01

Lock Down Your "Top 5" Metric Definitions

  • The Problem: Hours wasted weekly in cross-departmental debates over conflicting metrics (*"Why doesn't Marketing's revenue match Finance?"*).
  • Immediate Action: Select the 5 core business metrics (e.g., Active Customer, ARR, Churn) and establish one official calculation formula in a central dictionary.
  • Expected Impact: Eliminates 2–3 hours of meeting friction and rework per employee each week.
Step 02

Enforce "Data Ownership at the Source"

  • The Problem: Flawed data enters through sales forms or CRM entries, forcing downstream analysts to clean up errors manually.
  • Immediate Action: Shift accountability upstream. Add strict form validation (dropdowns vs. free text) and assign initial quality responsibility to data creators.
  • Expected Impact: Reduces downstream manual data scrubbing time by up to 50%.
Step 03

Establish a Single "Authorized" Data Hub

  • The Problem: Employees waste time asking in Slack or searching drives for current files and final reports.
  • Immediate Action: Designate a single location as the *only* authorized source for decision-making data. Mandate that unapproved sources cannot be presented to leadership.
  • Expected Impact: Reclaims 1–2 hours per week per employee previously spent searching for records.
